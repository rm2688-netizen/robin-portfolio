Role:
Tools: CFD, turbulence models, mesh sensitivity analysis

### Project 2
Title: Noise Reduction of Nose Wheel Landing Gear Using Enhanced DBD Plasma Actuator
Description: Designed and modeled a Dielectric Barrier Discharge Plasma Actuator to reduce noise and drag on a Boeing 777 aircraft nose landing gear. Used CATIA-V5 to design the landing gear and ANSYS to simulate airflow around the nose wheel landing gear with DBD plasma actuators turned on, showing improved streamline flow and noise reduction.
Role:
Tools: CATIA-V5, ANSYS
